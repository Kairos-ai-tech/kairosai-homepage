01 — Our Story

Applied AI, Built With Purpose

Who we are, why we started, and where we're headed.

Our Passion

We believe AI should solve real, tangible problems — not just impressive demos. Our passion is bringing engineering rigor to industries the tech world often overlooks, starting with construction.

How We Started

Kairos.ai began as a small team obsessed with a simple idea: use AI to give people back their time. That search led us deep into manufacturing and construction workflows, where we kept finding the same bottleneck — slow, error-prone manual rebar takeoff. So we built iTech to fix it.

Where We're Headed

We want iTech to become the standard way AEC teams estimate rebar — and to keep building AI systems, backend infrastructure, and data platforms with the same rigor for teams who need it.

02 — How it works

From drawing to schedule

iTech runs the full rebar takeoff pipeline — no manual counting, no spreadsheet errors.

STEP 01

Upload drawings

Drop in structural drawings — PDF or CAD. iTech ingests plans, sections, and details.

STEP 02

AI detects rebar

Computer-vision models read bars, sizes, spacing, and laps directly from the drawings.

STEP 03

Estimate & schedule

Get accurate quantities, weights, and a bar-bending schedule ready to export.
