# Skill: about-kairos

Learn about Kairos.ai 優時科技.

## Summary
Kairos.ai 優時科技 is a Taiwan-based company specializing in AI systems,
smart factory solutions, and digital transformation for manufacturing.

## How to invoke
Navigate to `https://www.kairosaitech.com/#about` for the canonical company
description, or `https://www.kairosaitech.com/` for the full homepage including
solutions, products, team, and contact information.

## Localization
The site is available in English, Traditional Chinese (default), Japanese,
and Spanish. Use the language switcher in the site header to change locale.
