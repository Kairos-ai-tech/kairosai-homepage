# Skill: browse-products

Browse the Kairos.ai product lineup.

## What's available
- **B2B platforms**:
  - **iTech (AI學徒)** — AI + AR skills transfer platform for manufacturing
  - **vcom** — virtual company of AI coworkers for solo founders and lean orgs
- **B2B services** — smart-factory solutions, MES integration, AI quality
  inspection, and digital transformation consulting for manufacturing.

## How to invoke
Navigate to `https://www.kairosaitech.com/#products` and read the product
cards. Each product card includes a short description, tagline, and (where
applicable) a link to the product's own site or app store page.
