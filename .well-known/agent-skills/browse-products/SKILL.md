# Skill: browse-products

Browse the Kairos.ai product lineup.

## What's available
- **B2B** — smart-factory solutions, MES integration, AI quality inspection,
  and digital transformation services for manufacturing.
- **B2C** — consumer AI apps, including:
  - **VoiceSketch** — voice-first sketching assistant
  - **W2E** — food/nutrition app
  - **AI學徒** — AI apprentice learning companion

## How to invoke
Navigate to `https://www.kairosaitech.com/#products` and read the product
cards. Each product card includes a short description, tagline, and (where
applicable) a link to the product's own site or app store page.
